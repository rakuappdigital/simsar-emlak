Privacy Policy

Odd Estate · Rakuapp Digital · Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rakuapp Digital ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the Odd Estate mobile game ("the App").

No account required

Odd Estate does not require you to create an account or sign in with any personal credentials. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect limited technical data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — displays in-app advertisements and may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad delivery and measurement. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — processes in-app purchase transactions and receipt validation. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Game Center — used for leaderboards and achievements, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information.

Children

Odd Estate is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Local game progress can be erased at any time by deleting the App. To request deletion of any data held by our third-party service providers on your behalf, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to sivilpenguen@gmail.com.
