Talk, Bluff & Sell in Istanbul

Promotional Text:
Follow an old key to a house no one has seen. Solve side stories across the city, collect the oddities in your album, and find out who really lives behind the 55th door.

Description:
You're Estetan: broke, unproven, and one bad month away from losing your desk. Istanbul's real estate market doesn't care about your résumé. It cares whether you can read a room, spot a lie, and close before the customer walks.

Odd Estate is a dialogue-driven negotiation game where every showing is a psychological duel. Push too hard and suspicion spikes. Play it too safe and a rival agent takes the sale. Every line you choose shifts the balance: read the customer, manage the tension, and land the deal while it's still on the table.

54 HOUSES, 54 STORIES
From a damp studio with a sad history to a penthouse whose owner is definitely not telling you everything, every property comes with its own characters, secrets, and reasons to walk away.

SIDE STORIES ACROSS THE CITY
An elderly client hands you a key with a faded label. Your boss keeps dropping hints about a house from his childhood. The neighborhood headman has a notebook full of small favors. Follow these threads from house to house and they open doors the regular listings never will.

SECRETS WORTH HUNTING
A haunted flat with a very ordinary explanation. A midnight client who only asks whether the curtains block the light. A council of seven cats. Every oddity you uncover goes into your album, and the empty pages leave you a hint. Some secrets only appear on special days of the real calendar.

FROM INTERN TO PARTNER
Every commission moves you up the office ladder. Each rank unlocks new tools, new leverage, and new ways to close.

FIVE RIVALS IN YOUR WAY
Other agents want the same clients. Outmaneuver them, or watch them take your sale.

PEOPLE REMEMBER YOU
Customers come back, friends call, and the choices you made months ago come up again. Honest or cunning, your reputation follows you.

FLIP FOR PROFIT
Unlock the investment market: buy low, renovate, sell high.

YOUR OWN STYLE
Pick a backstory, grow a skill tree, and find your voice: charming, sincere, or bold. Small mini-games fill your days between showings.

MANY ENDINGS
Open your own office, become your boss's partner, or get fired. How your career ends depends on how you played it.

Try the first houses for free, then unlock the full career with a one-time purchase.

Odd Estate isn't about spreadsheets. It's about people: reading them, understanding them, and knowing exactly when to push and when to let them come to you.
