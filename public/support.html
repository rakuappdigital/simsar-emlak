Frequently asked

  • I made a purchase but didn't receive it. Try "Restore Purchases" in the Settings menu. If it still doesn't appear, email us with your purchase receipt.
  • The game is not saving my progress. Progress is stored locally on your device — make sure the App has not been reinstalled or the device storage cleared.
  • How do I remove ads? Ad removal is available as an in-app purchase from the Settings menu.
